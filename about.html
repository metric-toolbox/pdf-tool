About FreePDFHub

Last updated: October 31, 2026

FreePDFHub exists because “free” stopped meaning free. Somewhere along the way, online PDF tools turned into funnels: upload your file, then hit a sign-up wall, a daily limit, a watermark, or a credit card form for a “free trial”.

We built FreePDFHub on three promises:

  • Free forever. Every tool — merge, split, compress, convert, rotate — is completely free with no premium tier, no limits, and no credit card field anywhere on this site.
  • No accounts. We don't have a sign-up form. We don't want your email. You arrive, you fix your PDF, you leave.
  • Privacy by architecture. Our tools run entirely in your browser using open-source JavaScript libraries. Your files are never transmitted to our servers — we couldn't read them if we wanted to.

How can it be free?

Because your own device does the work. Traditional PDF sites pay for servers to process millions of files, and those costs become your subscription. Our processing cost per file is zero, so the price can be too.

Who is it for?

Students combining lecture notes, job seekers compressing CVs under upload limits, offices fixing sideways scans, freelancers converting invoices — anyone who touches a PDF and refuses to pay $20/month for a merge button.
