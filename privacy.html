Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version

Your files never leave your device. All PDF processing on FreePDFHub happens locally in your web browser using JavaScript. We do not upload, receive, store, scan or back up your documents — ever.

1. Files and documents

When you use any tool (merge, split, compress, convert, rotate), the file is read by your browser's memory and processed on your device. No document content is transmitted to our servers or any third party.

2. Accounts and personal data

We have no user accounts, no sign-up forms, and we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or payment information. There is nothing to breach because we hold nothing.

3. Technical data

Like virtually all websites, our hosting provider may log standard, anonymous technical information (such as page requests and browser type) to keep the service running and secure. This data is not linked to your identity or your documents.

4. Cookies

FreePDFHub does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Third-party libraries

Our tools use open-source libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, jsPDF) loaded from public CDNs. These libraries run locally in your browser and do not transmit your files.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. The core promise — files never leave your device — will not change.
