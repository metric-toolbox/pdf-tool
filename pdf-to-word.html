Frequently Asked Questions

Everything people ask about our free pdf to word tool.

How do I convert PDF to Word for free without email?

Upload your PDF to this tool and click Convert — your editable Word file downloads instantly. We never ask for an email, account or payment.

Will the Word file keep my formatting?

The tool preserves the text content and paragraph flow. Complex layouts (columns, tables, images) may need light re-formatting after conversion.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

Scanned PDFs contain images, not text, so direct conversion isn't possible. Use an OCR tool first, or export pages as images with our PDF to JPG converter.

Does the converted file open in Google Docs?

Yes — upload the .doc file to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs to edit it online.

Is my PDF uploaded to your servers?

No. Text extraction happens 100% inside your browser; your document never leaves your device.
