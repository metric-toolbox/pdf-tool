Frequently Asked Questions

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Remove the password first (open the PDF with its password and print-to-PDF), then merge the unlocked copies.

Does merging reduce quality?

No — pages are copied as-is, with fonts, images and links intact.

Because processing uses your own device, even very large files work; speed depends on your hardware.
