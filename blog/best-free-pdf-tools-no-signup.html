10 Best Free PDF Tools Online — No Sign-up Required (2026)

By the FreePDFHub Team · Last updated: October 31, 2026

“Free” PDF websites have a dirty secret: most are free until the moment you click download — then come the sign-up forms, daily limits and watermarks. This list covers 10 genuinely free PDF tasks you can do online in 2026 with no account, no credit card and no watermark.

1. Merge PDF

Combine unlimited PDFs into one file. The FreePDFHub merger runs in your browser, so files are never uploaded. Best for: job applications, scanned batches, reports.

2. Split PDF

Extract pages with a simple range like 1-3,5. Split PDF free — output as one file or one PDF per page.

3. Compress PDF

Shrink files for email limits. The compressor offers three levels, including an Extreme mode for “max 1MB” upload forms.

4. PDF to Word

Get editable text out of a PDF without giving an email address — a rarity among converters.

5. JPG to PDF

Bundle phone photos of documents into one professional PDF with the image to PDF converter.

6. PDF to JPG

Export pages as sharp 2× resolution images for slides and social media: PDF to JPG.

7. Rotate PDF

Fix sideways scans permanently (not just in your viewer) with Rotate PDF.

8–10. Combine the Tools

The real power is chaining them — split out the pages you need, rotate the sideways ones, merge everything back, then compress for email. Four tasks, zero cost, zero accounts.

How to Spot a Fake “Free” PDF Site

  • It asks for an email “to send your file” — your file becomes marketing bait.
  • It shows a timer or “1 of 2 free tasks used today”.
  • The download has a watermark unless you “upgrade”.
  • It wants a credit card for a free trial.
