How to Compress a PDF for Free

Email providers and job portals often reject attachments over a few MB. Our free PDF compressor reduces PDF file size right in your browser — pick a compression level, click once, and download a lighter file. No sign-up, no payment, no watermark, no daily limit.

Smart Compression Levels

  • Low compression: maximum visual quality — great for portfolios and print.
  • Recommended: the sweet spot for email attachments and online uploads.
  • Extreme: smallest possible size — perfect when a form says “max 1MB”.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything people ask about our free compress pdf tool.

How can I compress a PDF to under 1MB for free?

Upload your PDF, choose the Extreme compression level and click Compress. For most scanned or image-heavy documents this brings the file under 1MB. It's free with no sign-up.

Does compressing a PDF reduce quality?

Compression re-encodes page images at a lower quality. The Recommended level keeps documents crisp and perfectly readable; Extreme trades some sharpness for the smallest size.

Is it safe to compress sensitive PDFs online?

On FreePDFHub, yes — the file is compressed inside your browser and never uploaded anywhere, so sensitive documents stay on your device.

Why did my PDF not get much smaller?

Text-only PDFs are already very efficient. The biggest savings come from scanned pages and photos inside PDFs.

Is there a limit on file size or number of compressions?

No. Compress as many PDFs as you like, free, with no registration or credit card.
